About

About me

I am an aspiring game and interactive application developer with a strong interest in creating socially impactful digital experiences.

During my degree studies, I collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to deliver a range of interactive applications, including a custom desktop game developed using a custom game engine, a mobile application prototype for children undergoing speech therapy, and a standalone mobile e-learning simulation application for SBS Transit as part of my Capstone Project and Integrated Work Study Programme.

In the final year of my diploma studies, I completed a six-month internship developing a visual novel serious game focused on raising awareness of depression. I was involved in the full game development lifecycle, from ideation and design to implementation, and gained practical experience applying programming skills and game development methodologies in a real-world project.

Through my diploma and degree education, I have developed proficiency in multiple programming languages and game development workflows. I am motivated to contribute my skills to professional teams and am particularly interested in opportunities within interactive application and game development, with a focus on serious games.

Thank you for exploring my games and interactive projects. I look forward to connecting and discussing potential opportunities
